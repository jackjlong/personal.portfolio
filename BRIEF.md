## Visual direction under exploration

Jack likes bold color, spatial navigation, layered or tilted cards, and motion with depth. Orbit's clickable areas and Deck's stacks are possible ingredients, not selected layouts. He wants to see independent choices from the Grokbot design team. Avoid a literal path, generic AI-sounding headlines, and making his signature the hero. The small signature screenshot is a style reference, not a production image. Candidate copy remains unapproved until Jack edits it.

Build concept prototypes in Git and discuss them in [the shared design issue](https://github.com/jackjlong/personal.portfolio/issues/1). Keep the final design decision with Jack. GitHub Pages is the intended first host after review; a custom domain may follow later.
